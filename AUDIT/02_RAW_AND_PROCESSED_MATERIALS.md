# Layer 2 — Raw and Processed Materials

## Role in the physical architecture

Layer 2 supplies the physical substances used by every hardware layer. It is an upstream industrial system, not a single box inside the data centre. Its outputs enter energy equipment, semiconductors, servers, networks, buildings, batteries, sensors and actuators.

## 1. Current chain

Extraction → Refining and separation → Purification to electronic grade → Transformation → Qualified into the infrastructure stack

## 2. Main problems and missing components

### The extraction claim is incorrect

Extraction can be a chokepoint. Concentration may occur at the reserve, mine, refining, technology, transport or political level. Some materials are recovered mainly as by-products, so supply cannot respond independently to their own price.

### Qualification is a gate, not a universal final stage

Qualification occurs at different points and can be customer-, process- or application-specific. A chemical qualified for one wafer process is not automatically qualified for another.

### Missing stages

- Exploration and resource definition
- Project development, permitting and financing
- Beneficiation and concentration
- Smelting or chemical conversion
- Precursor production
- High-purity processing
- Engineered-material production
- Logistics and inventory
- Customer/process qualification
- Scrap recovery, recycling and end-of-life treatment

### Missing material families

- Copper, aluminium and electrical steel
- Silicon metal, polysilicon and semiconductor wafers
- Quartz and crucible materials
- Photoresists, wet chemicals and specialty gases
- Gallium, germanium, indium, tantalum and tungsten
- Rare-earth elements and permanent-magnet alloys
- Graphite, lithium, nickel and other battery materials
- Glass preforms and optical-fibre materials
- Polymers, insulation and dielectric materials
- Cement, structural steel and construction aggregates
- Coolants, refrigerants and dielectric fluids

### Statements to change

- Replace “reserves are not the chokepoint” with a material-specific analysis.
- Do not treat “rare earths” as one homogeneous commodity. Separate individual elements, separation, metal/alloy production and magnet manufacture.
- Avoid saying that one country “controls” a material without defining whether the reference is mining, refining, equipment, intellectual property or exports.

## 3. Recommended structure and corrections

Exploration → Permitting and financing → Extraction/recovery → Beneficiation → Refining/separation → High-purity processing → Precursors/engineered materials → Usable product form → Customer qualification → Recycling/recovery

Group materials by functional destination:

1. Energy and grid materials
2. Semiconductor-grade inputs
3. Optical and communications materials
4. Data-centre construction and cooling materials
5. Battery and power-electronics materials
6. Robot structures, joints, sensors and actuators

## Connections to other layers

- **Layer 1:** materials become transformers, cables, generators, batteries and cooling equipment.
- **Layer 3:** electronic-grade materials and gases enter semiconductor manufacturing.
- **Layer 4:** metals, substrates, PCBs, connectors and optical materials become computing systems.
- **Layer 5:** steel, concrete, copper and cooling materials become facilities.
- **Layer 6:** glass, compound semiconductors and copper become communications networks.
- **Layer 10:** structures, magnets, batteries, bearings and sensors become autonomous machines.

## Website wording

> Layer 2 converts geological resources and industrial feedstocks into qualified materials with the purity, form and reliability required by the AI infrastructure stack.

## Required verification

For every concentration or shortage claim, identify the precise product, processing stage, geography, year, market share, substitute and source.
