# Changelog

What changed in each public revision of Road to AGI, written for readers rather
than developers. Commit-by-commit detail is in the git history.

A revision gets its number when it is published. Everything under
**Unreleased** becomes the next revision; moving it under a new heading is a
step in `docs/RELEASE.md`.

## Unreleased — since revision 10

### The overview map

- The hand-drawn schematic is replaced by an interactive map of the stack,
  laid out as six macro-systems from foundations to embodied AI, with the
  physical world beside them.
- Click any box for its role, headline metric, binding constraint, what to
  watch and its key companies. The physical world opens a panel of its own.
- Pointing at a box highlights its inputs, its outputs and the boxes at either
  end. The data centre and the machine at the edge are enclosures: pointing at
  one lights everything inside it.
- Search the map by company name or ticker.
- Filter by flow — energy, materials, compute, data, intelligence, control.
  Each filter is a switch: click it again to see the whole map.
- An arrow takes the colour of the layer it leaves; its dash pattern says what
  it carries.

### The layers

- Layers 6 to 9 are renumbered: 6 is now Data and knowledge infrastructure,
  7 AI models and inference, 8 Agentic software and applications, and
  9 Connectivity and communications. They were 7, 8, 9 and 6.
- The measurement framework in each layer description is clarified.

### The site

- A refreshed visual theme across every page.
- Every page carries a summary of the ten layers that reads without JavaScript.
- Each page loads only the data it uses.
- A site icon, and complete descriptions and previews when a page is shared.
- About, Disclaimer and Privacy pages, linked from the footer. The disclaimer
  states that the author holds investments in companies named here and works
  at one of them, and that AI tools help write the report.

## Revision 10 — 9 September 2026

- Ten layers, from energy to embodied AI.
- Company marks are hosted with the site rather than fetched from elsewhere,
  and a market map is drawn from the company database.
- The layer views are split across two pages, and value chains open one at
  a time.
- Every layer description carries a measurement framework.
- Market capitalisations are dated.
- The site publishes only after its checks pass, and carries a licence.
