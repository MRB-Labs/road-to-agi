Privacy

This site collects nothing about you. This page explains the few things that still touch your data, and who is responsible for them.

Who is responsible

Your name, and a contact address for privacy questions.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no cookie set by this site. There is no account, form or newsletter.

One setting, on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is saved in your browser’s local storage, under the name rtagi-theme, so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser’s data for this site removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, and may keep them in its logs for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Market data

Opening a company’s market data loads charts from TradingView. Only then does your browser contact TradingView, which receives your IP address and may set cookies of its own. Nothing is loaded from TradingView until you open one. See the TradingView privacy policy.

Links to sources

The report links to the sources it cites. Following a link takes you to a site with its own privacy practices.

Your rights

If you live somewhere with data-protection law, such as the EU or the UK, you have rights over personal data held about you. This site holds none, but you can write with any question to the contact above.
