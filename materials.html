Five materials gate everything

Underneath every manufacturing chokepoint sits a material one, and it is usually more concentrated. The instinct is to focus on mining; that instinct is wrong. For nearly every critical input, mining is geographically diverse and refining is not.

Where each material enters the infrastructure stack

Material chokepoints behave differently from manufacturing ones. A refinery can be replicated in three to eight years; ASML's accumulated engineering cannot. Material constraints shape the near-term timing of bottlenecks but should not, on their own, underwrite a decade-long thesis. The exception is China's export ban on rare earth separation technology — licensing the metal is a tariff, banning the know-how is a moat.
