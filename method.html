Method and sources

Facts are cited to named sources. Estimates and derived calculations are labelled as such. Opinions are separated from both.

How layers were defined

Ordered by physical dependency and by where the profit pool sits, not by sector classification. Chokepoints assessed on supplier concentration, substitutability, and the ratio of outstanding demand to installed capacity. Moat durability assessed by switching cost and by observed competitive change over the last three years.

Primary sources

Epoch AI (gigawatt data centre total cost of ownership). Company filings and earnings materials from GE Vernova, ASML, TSMC, NVIDIA, Broadcom, Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon. Counterpoint Research, IDC, Dell'Oro, TrendForce, Wood Mackenzie, ICSG, Morgan Stanley. Clark Hill and CIRS Group on export controls. getdeploying and BenchLM on pricing. Peer-reviewed and preprint robotics literature on vision-language-action inference. Generative Value for the wafer-fabrication process sequence, semiconductor capital equipment positions and data centre cost composition (“A Primer on Semiconductor Capital Equipment”, “An Overview of the Semiconductor Industry”, “A Primer on Data Centers”).

Known weak points

Global precision reducer capacity is my own derivation from industrial robot installation volumes, and the fiftyfold headline rests entirely on it — verify against a primary source before use. Private-company revenue estimates vary by a factor of five across sources and are unusable for valuation. Bill-of-materials breakdowns are analyst estimates, not disclosures. Equipment quantities and workforce figures are engineering estimates scaled from published benchmarks.
